# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate professionally, assume good faith, focus criticism on the work, and protect the privacy of people and projects represented in reports or examples.

Acceptable participation includes:

- giving specific, evidence-based feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- respecting different levels of experience and different product contexts;
- using synthetic or properly redacted examples;
- helping keep discussions relevant and actionable.

Unacceptable participation includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing private project data, deliberately misleading compliance claims, or pressuring others to disclose credentials or confidential evidence.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and should prioritize safety and privacy.

Report sensitive conduct privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** channel. Do not expose personal or confidential information in a public issue.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.
